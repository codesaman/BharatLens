Mathematical Formulation of the BharatLens Score (0–100 Scale)

The BharatLens Score is an interpretable, reproducible index scaled strictly from 0 to 100, synthesizing 5 primary socioeconomic dimensions using empirical public data:

Dimension Weight ($w_d$) Tracking Indicators Directional Polarity
1. Health & Nutrition 25% (0.25) Institutional births, Full immunization, 4+ ANC visits, Stunting (reversed), Wasting (reversed), Anaemia (reversed) Positive for care access; Inverted for burden rates
2. Women Empowerment 20% (0.20) Female literacy, 10+ years schooling, Rural female MGNREGA labour participation Positive (Higher = Better)
3. Infrastructure & Living Amenities 20% (0.20) Household electricity, Improved sanitation, Clean cooking fuel, Internet access Positive (Higher = Better)
4. Economic & Employment Floor 20% (0.20) MGNREGA daily wage (₹), Workdays provided per household, Workforce participation rate, Agricultural works expenditure Positive (Higher = Better)
5. Demographic & Educational Baseline 15% (0.15) Decadal literacy rate, Sex ratio (females per 1000 males), Urbanization rate Positive (Higher = Better)

Directional Min-Max Normalization:

Positive Indicators: Score_norm = ((x - min(x)) / (max(x) - min(x))) * 100
Burden Indicators: Score_norm = ((max(x) - x) / (max(x) - min(x))) * 100

Peer-Relative Momentum Z-Score ($Z_{\Delta}$):

To overcome national baseline drift (where nationwide schemes lift all raw figures simultaneously), growth momentum is measured relative to national and state peer groups:

Z_delta = (Delta_Score - Mean_National_Delta) / Std_National_Delta
  • Improving: Above-average momentum ($Z_{\Delta} \ge +0.35$ or relative state growth $\ge +1.5$ points).
  • Stable: Consistent tracking with benchmark momentum ($-0.35 \le Z_{\Delta} < +0.35$).
  • Declining: Below-average momentum ($Z_{\Delta} < -0.35$, falling behind peer pace).
  • Anomalous: Multi-variate outlier flagged by Isolation Forest ($contamination = 0.05$).

Ethical AI & Research Disclaimers:

• Association vs. Causation: All model features and SHAP explanations represent statistical associations learned across empirical observations. They do not assert deterministic legal or causal responsibility.
• Administrative Boundaries: Districts correspond to the 640-district framework mapped across Census 2011 and NFHS survey cycles. Newly bifurcated districts inherit apportioned baselines from parent units.
