BharatLens ML Portal

An empirical district and state socioeconomic intelligence platform processing 643 Indian districts across 37 States and Union Territories. Powered by machine learning clustering, anomaly detection, regression forecasting, and SHAP explainability.

Important Notice: BharatLens ML is an independent academic, econometric, and public research project based on open datasets. It is NOT an official portal of the Government of India or any Ministry.
Open Data Sources
National Family Health Survey (NFHS-4 / NFHS-5) Census of India (Primary Census Abstract 2011) Ministry of Rural Development (MGNREGA) India Meteorological Department (IMD) Open Government Data Platform (data.gov.in)
Architecture & Governance
• Framework: Flask 3.1 + Bootstrap 5 + Chart.js / Plotly
• Clustering: K-Means ($k=4$, Silhouette 0.206)
• Anomaly Detection: Isolation Forest (Contamination 5%)
• Trend Model: Ridge Champion ($R^2=0.998$, RMSE=0.186)
• Explainability: SHAP Linear & Tree Attribution Engine
